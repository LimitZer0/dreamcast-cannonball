Make a CannonBall (OutRun) disc for your Dreamcast

1. Your OutRun ROMs

Drop your OutRun (revision B) ROM zip here, or the ROM files themselves. File names don't matter; each file is checked by its contents. ROMs aren't included: you need your own.

2. Your music (optional)

Replace any of the four tunes with your own songs (MP3, OGG, WAV, FLAC or M4A). In the game, choose SETTINGS › SOUND › MUSIC SOURCE. Tunes you leave empty play the original music.

    3. Make the disc

    Download cannonball.cdi

    Play it in Flycast, copy it to a GDEMU (or similar), or burn it to a CD-R. Your settings and high scores save to the memory card.
